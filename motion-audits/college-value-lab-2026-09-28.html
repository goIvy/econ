MOTION AUDIT · DESIGN-MOTION-PRINCIPLES

college-value-lab: calm instrument, one orchestrated moment

College Value Lab is a marketing homepage plus research-grade calculators for students and families. Motion should read as a precise instrument responding, not a page performing.

Next.js 16 · React 19 · Framer Motion 13 · weighting set by the brief: Jakub primary, Jhey secondary
1Critical 3Important 4Opportunities 4 / 4Critical + Important fixed
OVERALL

The instrument moments are the strength: trace lines draw, readouts count, markers slide along their scales, and the break-even point settles in last. The first pass diluted that by giving every heading and paragraph the same blur-rise entrance and staggering three lists on one page. Those are now removed. Motion lives where the data changes, the hero keeps its single orchestrated entrance, and every variant sits in one file.

02 · WHERE THE TIMINGS LAND

Duration budget

  1. Hover tooltips, 120ms fade
  2. Crossfades (tabs, swaps), 200ms
  3. Micro springs (buttons, toggles, tray), 300ms
  4. Disclosures and sheets, 400ms spring
  5. Default enter (hero, results), 450ms spring
  6. Readout count-up, 600ms ease-out
  7. Trace line draw, 900ms ease-out
  8. Break-even marker settle, 500ms after a 900ms delay

What's off: nothing in the utility band. The two long ones (6 and 7) run only when a result changes, and 8 is the signature moment. All three retarget cleanly if inputs change mid-flight.

03 · PRIMARY LENS

Jakub Krehel — Production Polish Primary

WHAT'S WORKING WELL

  • Brief's enter recipe (opacity + 8px + 4px blur, spring 0.45 / bounce 0) kept for the hero and result panels only: lib/animations.ts › enter
  • Exits are shorter and quieter than entrances (120–220ms, smaller travel): enter.exit, collapse.exit, pop.exit

  • Cards lift with shadow on interaction instead of scale: components/ui/button.tsx, features/college/college-card.tsx
